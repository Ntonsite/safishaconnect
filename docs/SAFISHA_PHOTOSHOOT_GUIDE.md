# SafishaCon original photography guide

## Direction

Photograph actual consenting providers and customers in believable Dar es Salaam homes, apartments and offices. Use daylight, warm realistic skin tones, neutral backgrounds and restrained green accents. Show skill and independence. Avoid staged subservient poses, mansions, fake customer endorsements and visible private documents. Obtain written model/property releases and permission for any branding.

## Production notes

- Wardrobe: clean short-sleeve workwear / simple overalls, closed shoes and task-appropriate gloves. Photograph safe working practices and real equipment.
- Light: window daylight or shaded outdoor light; avoid hard midday facial shadows, extreme HDR and oversaturated edits.
- Background: tidy lived-in homes and ordinary offices; remove personal addresses, bills, family portraits and customer identifiers.
- Capture landscape 3:2, portrait 4:5 and a mobile-safe 4:3 alternative. Keep face, hands and primary tool inside the central 70%; capture equipment fully in a separate frame.
- For optional future text overlays, leave roughly 40% clean negative space on the left and shoot a mirrored composition. The current site keeps text beside photographs.
- Deliver high-resolution originals (at least 3000px long edge), selected crop guides, creator credits, release references and natural-color edits. Export responsive WebP variants for the website through the central imagery config.
